A pub quiz night, a trivia game at a birthday party, a quick round of questions to close out an event: all of it gets easier when everyone answers on their own phone. You put a **QR code** on the table, people point their camera at it, answer and land on the **leaderboard**. No paper, no pens and no app to download.

In this guide you will set up a quiz like that on QuestioFlux, keep it **private** so only the people in the room play, and run the round from start to finish.

## Before you start: how it works

- Everyone answers **at their own pace**, on their own phone. It is not a synced big-screen game like Kahoot!. For a round with a set time, you agree on a deadline (see step 5).
- The leaderboard puts **whoever gets the most right** on top. Ties go to **whoever finished faster**.
- Players **don't need an account**. Without one, they type a name at the end, and that name is what shows on the leaderboard.
- The leaderboard has **two tables**: one for people who played with an account and one for guests. Players see the top 10 of each; you, as the quiz creator, see everyone.

## Step by step

### 1. Create a trivia quiz

Create your free account, click **Create quiz** and choose **[Trivia quiz](/en/create/quiz/questions-and-answers)**, the type with a leaderboard. Write the questions: in the editor, **the first option is always the right one**, and the site shuffles the order when people play.

On the free plan, each quiz has up to 10 text-only questions. For a whole night, run **rounds**: one 10-question quiz per topic ("Round 1: music", "Round 2: movies"), each with its own QR code. Picture rounds (guess the logo, name the album cover) need QuestioPlus or a one-time pass.

### 2. Make the quiz private

On the quiz cover, choose **private**. A private quiz doesn't show up in Explore or on Google: only people with the link can play. That way, nobody outside the venue lands on your night's leaderboard.

### 3. Create the QR code

On the quiz page, click **Share Quiz**, then **QR code**. It shows up right away, and the **Download QR code** button saves it as a PNG, ready to print.

The QR code opens the game directly: whoever scans it starts playing. Since the quiz is private, only people with the QR code or the link can get in. **Test it before printing:** scan it with two different phones and make sure it opens the right quiz.

### 4. Put the QR code where people can see it

Print it on table tents, stick it on the bar, put it on an easel at the door or show it on the TV. Next to the QR code, write the rules in one line, for example: "Play as a guest, once per person, until 10 pm. Use your table's team name."

### 5. Set the round's rules

Three rules prevent confusion:

- **Everyone plays as a guest.** Accounts and guests sit in separate tables, so asking everyone to play as a guest puts the whole room on one leaderboard.
- **Once per person (or per team).** Every time a guest plays again, it counts as a new entry on the leaderboard. Agree that the first one counts.
- **A deadline.** Announce when the round closes. At that time, open the leaderboard and take a screenshot: anyone who plays later still shows up, but doesn't count for the prize.

### 6. Show the leaderboard and hand out prizes

Sign in, open the quiz's leaderboard and put it on the TV or read it out loud. As the creator, you see every entry, not just the top 10. For several rounds, add up each team's positions at the end of the night.

## Ideas for every kind of event

- **Bar or restaurant:** 2000s music, sports or general knowledge trivia, with one round per week and a prize for the winning table.
- **Birthday or baby shower:** "How well do you know the guest of honor?". For that topic, the [about me quiz](/en/create/quiz/about-me) also has a leaderboard.
- **Wedding:** questions about the couple's story, with the QR code on the table card.
- **Company event or training:** five questions about what was presented, to wrap up the talk.
- **Classroom:** a review before the test, with the QR code projected on the board.

## Other formats that work with a QR code

The same way of sharing (private link, QR code on the table) works with the other formats:

- **[Personality quiz](/en/create/quiz/personality)** as an icebreaker: "What kind of party guest are you?". There are no right answers or leaderboard here, and people can play without typing a name.
- **[Favorites tournament](/en/create/experiences/tournament)** for the crowd to vote on: the house cocktail, the next song, the best costume of the night.
- **[Compatibility test](/en/create/experiences/compatibility)** for a couples' or friends' night: each person answers and compares answers with someone else.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do players need to download an app or create an account?**
No. The phone camera opens the link, and people play as guests, typing only a name at the end.

**Can people outside the event play?**
Only if they get the link. A private quiz doesn't show up in Explore or in search results. If the QR code gets passed around, create a new quiz for the next round.

**Can I see every player, not just the top 10?**
Yes, if you created the quiz. Players see the top 10 of each table.

**Is it a live game, with the questions on a big screen?**
No. Everyone answers on their own phone, at their own pace. If you need the whole room answering the same question at the same time on a big screen, a live game tool such as Kahoot! is a better fit.
